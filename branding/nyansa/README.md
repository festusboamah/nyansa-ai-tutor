# Nyansa visual identity concept

This folder contains a warm, learning-led logo direction for Nyansa.

## Concept

The symbol combines an open book, a lowercase **n**, and a small spark of insight. The simple silhouette is designed to remain readable at favicon size.

## Palette

- Terracotta: primary brand colour
- Golden ochre: insight accent
- Charcoal: wordmark and neutral contrast

Purple, blue, cyan, gradients, robot heads, brains, and circuit motifs were intentionally avoided.
