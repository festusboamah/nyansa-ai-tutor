2. What Nyansa is

Nyansa is a teaching and learning platform for schools in Ghana. Its core is an AI tutor for students, a lesson planning and grading copilot for teachers, and mastery evidence that shows what a student actually understands. Some schools also use Nyansa's attendance, fee collection, guardian communication, and term report tools, though these are being progressively handed over to Suku360, a partner school operations platform run by the same company, as described in Section 7.

3. Accounts and access

A school's administrator manages staff accounts and access levels within that school's workspace. Teachers, students, and parents or guardians each see only what their role needs. Each person is responsible for keeping their own login credentials confidential and for activity that happens under their account.

4. AI generated content and human review

Nyansa uses AI to tutor students, draft lesson notes, generate quiz and question bank items, and offer grading feedback. This output is advisory by default. A teacher or administrator must review and approve anything an AI produces before it becomes part of a grade, a report, or a permanent student record. Tutor sessions are logged so teachers and administrators can review what a student was told.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Schools subscribe to Nyansa under a plan agreed at signup, typically starting with a trial period and invoiced at the end of each billing period. Where a school enables online fee payments to itself, parent and guardian payments are processed by a third party payment provider and settle to the school's own account; Nyansa does not hold or take a cut of those funds. Subscription fees paid by the school to Nyansa are processed by the same third party payment provider, which is shared with Suku360, the partner platform mentioned in Section 7, since both are operated by the same company.

This linkage only happens for schools that explicitly enable it. See our Privacy Policy for the specific data involved.
